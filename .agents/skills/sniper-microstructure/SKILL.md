# Sniper Microstructure and Degen Market Research

Read `docs/THREAT_MODEL.md`, `harness/docs/MEMECOIN_RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md` and official Solana transaction landing documentation.

## Workflow
1. Study early creator/bundled buys, independent buyer breadth, net flow, creator inventory changes, realistic whole-position exit liquidity and price impact.
2. Identify source-of-edge hypotheses for immediate entry, fixed delay, flow confirmation, dip recovery, curve progression and post-migration trading. Explain which facts are available when an independent bot could decide.
3. Distinguish observable successful addresses from complete P&L and replicable strategies. Wallet copying requires transfers, open holdings, fees, and creator clustering—not headline leaderboards.
4. Include documented transaction send, network fee/tip, MEV and delayed-landing economics; falsify each technique on an unbiased entire-launch cohort.
5. Treat wash trading, manipulative pumping, fabricated volume and sandwich tactics as adversarial behavior to detect, not trading recommendations.

## Required deliverable
Hypothesis card per technique: causal mechanism, phase, point-in-time features, abstention rules, execution assumptions, adverse-selection traps, negative controls.
